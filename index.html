How it works

Scan → activate → fly

Every Sentrick Drone ships with a unique SDR- barcode. That barcode is the key that links the aircraft to your account, our cloud, and your monthly plan.

Scan the barcode

The QR on the drone opens your Sentrick activation page.

Sign in

Log in or create your Sentrick account in seconds.

Bind to the cloud

The drone is registered to your account on the Sentrick cloud automatically.

Start your plan

Confirm the monthly subscription — secure checkout.

Fly & monitor

Your drone goes live. Flights, video, and alerts flow to your dashboard.
